# Duotone

One decision about colour for everything: the darks become one colour, the lights another, the tones between step evenly from one to the other. Detail and light are kept; only colour is replaced.

## 角色
调色 (colour): under structure and finish.

## 何时用
Only when the film needs one mood or one brand over pictures that don't share it.
- **Many sources, one brand** (stock footage, screen recordings and photos in one section): the brand's darkest and lightest colours.
- **Memory** (a flashback, where it started, a quiet look back): navy and bone, or oxblood and cream, contrast 1.3.
- **Emphasis** (one shot drops to two colours while a number or a quote comes up).
- **A ground for type** (footage toned down so words over it read).
- Words that call for it: duotone, two-colour, two-tone, monochrome, tinted, toned, in brand colours; 双色、双色调、单色、品牌色、做成…色.

## 适合
- Every kind of picture checked, low-key ones included: the safest filter here. Faces, products and a product's screen all hold.
- Pairs that work: navy #0e1a33 with bone #f3ead9 (default); oxblood #3a0a12 with cream #f6e7d8; forest #0b2e2a with butter #f2e8cf; near-black #05070d with ember #ff6a2b for night.

## 不适合
- Pictures where colour carries meaning: a chart with coloured series, a product in several colours, red and green for bad and good.
- A whole long film: the eye stops seeing it. A section, or a recurring device.

## 俗套
The tired version is a neon pair, hot pink with electric blue or purple with orange. Keep the dark colour really dark and the light one really light (a dark under a fifth of full brightness, a light over four fifths), and both a little muted.

## 参数
- `amount` 0–1: 1 is two colours; 0.5 tints the picture.
- `dark`, `light`: the two colours.
- `contrast` 0.5–2.5: 1.2–1.8 usual; higher leaves less of the tones between.
- `balance` 0–1: where dark gives way to light. 0.35 more dark; 0.65–0.75 more light, for a dark picture.
