# Grain

Takes the digital shine off: the picture gets a surface, like film or a good print. A little goes a long way.

## 角色
质感收尾 (finish): the last filter on top, over colour and structure.

## 适合
- Product films that feel too clean or too flat; dark, moody scenes.
- Pulling a mix of footage and drawn clips into one look.
- amount 0.15–0.3 for "a touch of grain", 0.4–0.7 for an obvious film look; size 1–2 fine, 3+ coarse.

## 不适合
- Small text and fine UI: grain makes it harder to read.
- A bright, crisp, high-tech look.
