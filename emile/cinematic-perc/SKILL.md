# Cinematic percussion

A concert bass drum hit hard, a timpani hit, a suspended cymbal swelling over two seconds, a bowed cymbal shimmer.

## Range

A kit: one sound per key.

- 35: bass drum
- 36: timpani
- 55: cymbal swell (2 s)
- 57: bowed cymbal

## Suits

A hit on a big cut or a title landing, a swell that ends exactly on a reveal (start it two seconds before), weight at the end of a film.

## Doesn't suit

A steady beat, more than a hit every few seconds.

## Playing it

Velocity 0–1 sets how hard a note is played. In a score, `instrument: 'cinematic-perc'`.
