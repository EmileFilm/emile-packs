# Wipe

A clean graphic change, like turning a page sideways. 0.3–0.6 s.

## 适合
- Lists, steps and comparisons: one thing after the next.
- Explainers and product films with a flat, designed look.

## 不适合
- Soft, painterly looks (ink, watercolour): the straight edge breaks them.
- A presenter in the middle of the frame: the edge crosses the face.
