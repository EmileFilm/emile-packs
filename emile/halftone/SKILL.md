# Halftone

Turns any picture into print: a screen of dots, darker areas in bigger dots, on warm paper.

## 角色
结构 (structure): it remakes the picture; put colour grading under it and a finish (grain) over it.

## 适合
- Editorial, zine and poster looks; retro, printed, hand-made feels.
- Portraits and bold shapes; a title card over a photo.
- cell 8–14 for a fine screen, 20–40 for big pop-art dots; mode colour keeps the picture's colours.

## 不适合
- Small text and detailed UI: dots break them up.
- Long stretches of a film: it tires the eye; use it for a section.
