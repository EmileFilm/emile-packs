# Dissolve

The quietest blend: the outgoing picture fades as the incoming one comes up. 0.3–0.8 s.

## 适合
- A change of scene with nothing to lead the eye from one to the other.
- Calm films, talk, a pause between chapters.

## 不适合
- Fast cuts on a beat: it reads as hesitation.
- Two pictures with text in the same place: the words overlap half way.
