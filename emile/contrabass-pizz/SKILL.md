# Double bass, plucked

A solo double bass plucked with the finger: a round low note that fades within a second or two.

## Range

MIDI 28–59. Notes outside it are refused.

## Suits

The bass under piano or strings, a walking line, roots on the beat in a light groove.

## Doesn't suit

Long sustained bass (use strings, cellos), anything above E3.

## Playing it

Velocity 0–1 sets how hard a note is played, and above about 0.63 a brighter recording takes over. In a score, `instrument: 'contrabass-pizz'`.
