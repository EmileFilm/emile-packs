# String section

Violin, viola and cello sections bowing long notes with vibrato, recorded soft: a quarter-second swell in, then a held tone. Cellos below C3, violas to about A4, violins above.

## Range

MIDI 36–86. Notes outside it are refused.

## Suits

A pad under the whole film, chords held across a cut, a slow line that swells into a reveal, a held high note over a quiet or black moment.

## Doesn't suit

Short rhythmic notes (use strings-spiccato), anything faster than about two notes a second.

## Playing it

Velocity 0–1 sets how hard a note is played. In a score, `instrument: 'strings'`.
