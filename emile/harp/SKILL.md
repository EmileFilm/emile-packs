# Concert harp

A concert harp plucked: bright attack, notes ring several seconds.

## Range

MIDI 28–101. Notes outside it are refused.

## Suits

Arpeggios that open a film or a section, a glissando into a reveal, a gentle, elegant mood.

## Doesn't suit

A driving rhythm, a dark or tense film.

## Playing it

Velocity 0–1 sets how hard a note is played, and above about 0.63 a brighter recording takes over. In a score, `instrument: 'harp'`.
