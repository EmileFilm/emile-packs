# Pink phosphor

A monochrome monitor whose tube glows pink. Laid, it is three filter clips, bottom to top, each on its own track over the same span: `emile/duotone` (the two tones), `emile/bloom` (the glow) and `emile/crt` (the tube). Each stays its own clip: tune it or take it off afterwards.

## When
- The old way of showing something: a terminal, a machine's own screen, a readout.
- A few seconds, as `emile/crt`'s guide says; over a whole film it tires.

## On what picture
- Best on a dark shot with a few lit things in it: the lit things become the glow.
- A bright daylight shot fills the screen with the colour and loses its shapes: darken it first, or choose another shot.
- Small type under it is lost in the scan lines: keep words large, or put them above the look's tracks.

## Another colour
The same three make any one-colour tube by changing duotone's two colours: green `light` `#7dff9a` with `dark` `#03120a`; amber `light` `#ffb347` with `dark` `#140a02`.
