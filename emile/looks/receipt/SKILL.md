# Receipt

A picture as a thermal printer makes it: black dots on off-white paper. Laid, it is one filter clip, `emile/dither`, in the till's ink and paper. Nothing goes over it.

## When
- A list, a price, a summary, an order: a still or a few seconds.
- Not for faces the film needs read: the dots take the expression.

## The slip itself
The slip is not part of the look: write it as a code clip. A tall narrow column of the paper colour (`#f5f2ea`) in the middle of a plain ground; monospaced capitals in the ink colour (`#1b1b1f`): a shop's name, a date and time, a few lines of items with amounts set flush right, a total, a row of dashes, a barcode drawn as bars; the paper's bottom edge torn in small teeth; the whole column feeding upwards a line at a time as if printing. Put the picture clip and this look under the code clip, the picture scaled to the column's width, so the dithered picture is one of the slip's blocks.
