# Direct flash finish

The look of a small camera's own flash at night: the person bright and flat, a hard shadow just behind them, the room falling away into black. Laid, it is two filter clips, bottom to top: `emile/chromatic-aberration` (a little lens fringing) and `emile/grain` (fine grain). They take the sheen off a shot that already has that light.

## The flash is light in the scene
No filter adds a flash to a shot lit another way: it is asked for when the shot is generated or photographed. In the prompt for the image or the video: direct on-camera flash, hard-edged shadow close behind the subject, bright foreground falling off fast into a dark background, slightly overexposed skin, a point-and-shoot snapshot at night, wide lens, everything in focus. Then lay this look over the generated shot.

## On a shot that already exists
Say that the flash itself can't be added afterwards, and offer this finish alone or a new generated shot.

## When
- Party, street and backstage pictures; a night out told as snapshots.
- Over the shots made that way only, not the whole film.
