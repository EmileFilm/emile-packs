# Zoom

Going deeper: into a detail, into the next idea. 0.4–0.8 s.

## Suits
- From an overview into one part of it.
- Energetic openings and reveals.

## Doesn't suit
- Text-heavy pictures: the type blurs as it scales.
- Several in a row: it tires the eye quickly.
