# Strings, short

Violin and cello sections playing short bounced notes (spiccato): a dry, quick pulse.

## Range

MIDI 36–86. Notes outside it are refused.

## Suits

An eighth-note pulse under a product shown in motion, building tension before a reveal, doubling a bass line an octave up.

## Doesn't suit

Held notes (use strings), slow quiet passages.

## Playing it

Velocity 0–1 sets how hard a note is played. In a score, `instrument: 'strings-spiccato'`.
