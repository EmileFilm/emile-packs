# Drum kit

An acoustic drum kit recorded from a middle distance: a full kick, a crisp snare, closed and open hi-hats, a crash.

## Range

A kit: one sound per key.

- 36: kick
- 38: snare
- 42: closed hi-hat
- 46: open hi-hat
- 49: crash

## Suits

A groove from the point a film picks up, a crash on a big cut, a fill into a reveal.

## Doesn't suit

A quiet or reflective film, electronic styles (use the generated music for those).

## Playing it

Velocity 0–1 sets how hard a note is played. In a score, `instrument: 'drums'`.
