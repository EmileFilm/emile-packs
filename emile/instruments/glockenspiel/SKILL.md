# Glockenspiel

A glockenspiel: very high, bright, short bell notes.

## Range

MIDI 79–108. Notes outside it are refused.

## Suits

A sparkle on a reveal or a logo, doubling a melody two octaves up, a playful or magical moment.

## Doesn't suit

More than a few notes at a time, anything under G5.

## Playing it

Velocity 0–1 sets how hard a note is played. In a score, `instrument: 'glockenspiel'`.
