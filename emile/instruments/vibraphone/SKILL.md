# Vibraphone

A vibraphone struck with soft or hard mallets: a clean, bell-like tone that rings.

## Range

MIDI 53–88. Notes outside it are refused.

## Suits

A light, clean tech or product film, a repeating figure over a pad, small accents on the cuts.

## Doesn't suit

Low notes (it starts at F3), a heavy or epic mood.

## Playing it

Velocity 0–1 sets how hard a note is played, and above about 0.63 a brighter recording takes over. In a score, `instrument: 'vibraphone'`.
