# Push

Movement onward: the film travels to the next place. 0.3–0.6 s.

## 适合
- A change of scene that goes forward in a story or a process.
- A swipe-like feel for mobile and product films.

## 不适合
- Going back to an earlier scene: the direction says "next".
- Pictures with fine detail at the edges, which smears on a fast push.
