# Grand piano

A Kawai grand piano recorded close: warm and round when played softly, brighter and ringing when hit hard (two layers, the change at velocity 0.63).

## Range

MIDI 22–108. Notes outside it are refused.

## Suits

Ostinatos and arpeggios under a calm or thoughtful film, a melody on top, chords that change on the cuts, single notes over a quiet moment.

## Doesn't suit

Fast dense runs in the low octaves (they muddy), a driving beat by itself.

## Playing it

Velocity 0–1 sets how hard a note is played, and above about 0.63 a brighter recording takes over. In a score, `instrument: 'piano'`.
