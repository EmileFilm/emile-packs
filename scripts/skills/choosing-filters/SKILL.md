# Choosing a filter

One page over the fourteen free filters, drawn from each pack's own guide: what each is for, how long it holds up, and where it fails. Choose here, then read the chosen pack's guide in full (`read_pack`) before placing or tuning it: its values are the ones to use.

## How to choose

1. **Start from the moment, not the filter.** Find the moment in the film that calls for a look (a headline that should feel published, a memory, the problem before the product, a hit on the beat) and the filter whose moments below match it. No moment calls for one: use none.
2. **Keep to its length.** Most filters are an accent of a few seconds over the moment that calls for them, between clean shots. With no span named, lay it over that moment only, as long as its How long says, and say where you put it. Only a light finish may run the whole film unasked.
3. **Check the picture against Fails on.** A dark shot, an interface, numbers or small type under a strong filter: use the guide's mending setting, move the words to a track above the filter clip, or choose another filter.
4. **Stack by role.** Colour goes lowest, a medium (`crt`, `vhs`) just under the finish, a finish (`grain`, `paper`) on top; each Role below says where it goes and what doesn't go with it (`paper` never with a print filter, nothing over `dither`).
